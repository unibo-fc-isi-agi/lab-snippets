# Drafting a letter to a candidate

1. Make sure you know the candidate's full name and the committee's decision: admission, rejection,
   or invitation to the interview (then, also date and time). If anything is missing, ask; never guess a decision.
2. Fill in `assets/letter-template.md`, keeping only the paragraph matching the decision.
3. Keep the tone formal and kind. Do not mention scores, other candidates, or the committee's internal discussions.
4. Show the draft to the committee: never send it.
